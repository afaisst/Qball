## Title
Aiming with a ghost ball

## Goals

* learn a basic aiming system using a ghost ball


## Script


1. Explain the concept of a ghost ball: imagine a ball that sits touching next to the object ball. The ghost ball is on the line from object ball to pocket. Imagine that the cueball has to replace the object ball. Therefore you have to aim at the center of the ghost ball. Because balls are not point sources but extended, you don't aim at the object, but the aiming point is offset.

2. Demonstrate the concept by a few shots.

First set-up: straight shot ( o=Object ball, c=cueball)

------------^------------
|           o 	   	|
|		   	|
|	  	   	|
|	    c	   	|
|		   	|
------------v------------

* cueball 2/3 lower table, center line. 
* object ball (number 9) upper 2/3 table, front of side pocket.
* both balls are in line for a straight shot in. 
* object ball is hit center (player cannot change)
* ghost ball visible
* predicted object ball line visible.
* no tangent line
* no cueball line after shot visible
* aiming already set up (player cannot move/drag anything)

- 2D view: Zoom in on Ghost ball, show line from ghost ball through object ball to pocket.
- Switch to 3D view (about 30% tilt). Camera moves, shows object ball and ghost ball (viewing direction pocket). -> show how alignment is and note that the ghost ball needs to be replaced by cue ball. Note that because balls are extended, aiming point is the center of the ghost ball.
- 3D view aligns with CB GB, OB, pocket. Say that in the case of a straight shot, the GB is in front of the OB, hence the CB hits center OB as seen from the CB.
- let the player shoot (player drags shot, but the strength fixed is such that the OB goes in and the CB stops in front of the pocket) in 3D aim view. Zoom out

- Player first is asked to drag the GB to the appropriate location.

## Summary:
Summarize what we have learned:
- ghost ball is a good aiming aid. Helps to visualize where player has to aim.
- Aiming on a point of the ball is difficult, therefore try to match the eclipse angle, which is easier and more accurate
- still need practice many balls to get good at angles.
